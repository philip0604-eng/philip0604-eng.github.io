Monetary Autonomy for Global Cities

State-Contingent Purchasing Power and Urban Economic Stabilization

DL
Donghyun LeeIndependent Researcher
DL / RESEARCH2026
D/L.

01ABSTRACT

Abstract

This paper develops a capacity-first architecture for limited urban stabilization within a common currency. National-money payments retain their face value; in demand-driven downturns, a nonredeemable, dated entitlement expands access to contracted services at unchanged nominal expenditure. The default payment rail is an existing card or regulated payment provider, not a municipal wallet. A separately branded, fully safeguarded balance is an optional institutional extension subject to an incremental welfare test. We derive discount equivalence, merchant participation, constrained feedback, risk-adjusted procurement and audit incentive conditions. Reverse procurement selects dated capacity on the basis of fulfillment cost, quality, counterfactual utilization and expected additional activity rather than advertised discounts. A national welfare criterion and a regional grant mechanism distinguish newly created trips, duration extensions, timing shifts and spatial diversion. Separate financial and service-commitment accounts protect redemption and fulfillment. Observed New York and Greater Paris tourism series and published empirical studies establish context; synthetic experiments illustrate the conditions under which the proposed procurement, payment and coordination modules may or may not outperform ordinary rebates. The policy is explicitly one-sided, inactive under binding physical or mobility constraints, and confined to sectors with verifiable spare capacity and favorable incremental economics. A staged experimental design tests the ordinary-payment protocol before considering a distinctive medium. The contribution is conditional institutional design rather than evidence of an already effective currency: where additional production cannot cover real resource, administrative, displacement and financing costs, the optimal intervention is zero.
