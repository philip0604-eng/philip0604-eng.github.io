01BOOK OVERVIEW

Book overview

This book examines how foundational human guarantees might be embedded in the protocols of economic coordination. Its institutional vision brings together purpose-oriented monetary arrangements, universal starting capital, intergenerational fairness, and governance of computational systems in an era of artificial intelligence. The argument begins from a tension between dynamic innovation and concentrated control over the economic rules through which people participate in markets. This editorial overview is based on the book’s preface; the full text is available in the PDF.
