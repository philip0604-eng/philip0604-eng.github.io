AI READING NOTE 01Monetary Design & Inflation

Begin with the inflation question

Monetary Design and Inflation↗

Why read it
Investigates whether the design of purchasing rights changes price formation when real capacity and settlement obligations are explicit.
Key contribution to examine
Separates the financial claim from available supply and tests price effects in constrained sectors.
Question to test
When do spillovers, supplier markups and settlement costs negate a promised inflation advantage?
READ ALONGSIDEPurpose-Bound Transfers, Idle Capacity, and Inflation↗

AI READING NOTE 02Persistent Inequality

Track the dynamics of disadvantage

Breaking the Cycle of Inequality↗

Why read it
Explains how financial wealth, health, housing and education form reinforcing chains of disadvantage over time.
Key contribution to examine
Distinguishes direct inequality from cross-domain transmission and evaluates interventions on those links.
Question to test
Which links can be causally identified with longitudinal household data?
READ ALONGSIDEMechanisms of Inequality↗

AI READING NOTE 03Institutions & Access

Start with effective access

Access Over Output↗

Why read it
Asks why a society can possess goods and capacity while households lack the purchasing channels needed to use them.
Key contribution to examine
Introduces supply-constrained access institutions as a complement to development through domestic production.
Question to test
How reliably could eligible supply and public liabilities be verified?

AI READING NOTE 04Financial Resilience

Study financial crisis transmission

A New Architecture for Financial Stability↗

Why read it
Connects declining collateral, squeezed operating cash flows and credit withdrawal in a unified financial-resilience framework.
Key contribution to examine
Contrasts countercyclical collateral support with targeted cash-flow insurance and explicit public guarantees.
Question to test
Which borrowers can be protected without subsidizing unviable firms or transferring losses to taxpayers?

AI READING NOTE 05Market Guarantees & Innovation

Make future markets credible

Transforming Climate Agreements↗

Why read it
Proposes treaty-backed advance commitments to qualified environmental technologies and a trade-off between innovation rents and competition.
Key contribution to examine
Links technical performance requirements, verified purchasing quantities, industrial learning and scale-sensitive contracts.
Question to test
When does limited exclusivity improve innovation incentives enough to outweigh concentration and implementation risks?

AI READING NOTE 06Democracy & Collective Choice

Consider collective choice under disagreement

The Economics of Preference Diversity↗

Why read it
Takes Arrow’s impossibility theorem seriously while modeling preference diversity, agenda formation, lobbying and consultation.
Key contribution to examine
Distinguishes combinatorial orderings from actual majority cycles and models strategic political participation.
Question to test
Can public consultation improve decisions without amplifying mobilization bias or undermining legislative accountability?
