This independent research collection considers how the architecture of money, capital, firms, and public institutions shapes economic opportunity. It spans theoretical and institutional manuscripts, empirical research designs, and philosophical inquiries.

The works are shared to invite scrutiny and discussion. They should be read as independent research manuscripts, not as claims of journal publication or peer review.
